Integrations

External APIs We Use

Frankfurter
https://api.frankfurter.dev
  • Live exchange rates and currency conversion
  • Budget dashboard and journey FX guidance
  • Cash exchange planning estimates
CountriesNow
https://countriesnow.space
  • Country, city, and currency reference data
  • Powers the trip creation form selectors
  • Validates destination and primary currency choices
OpenStreetMap
https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org
  • Nominatim — geocode location names to map coordinates
  • Overpass — search nearby ATMs for backup cash
  • Powers the ATM Finder map experience

Conclusion

What We Delivered

  • Product value — Guided overseas banking journeys: prepare before travel, track spending abroad, recover safely when things go wrong.
  • Technical outcome — Full-stack Spring Boot app with explainable rules, external integrations, responsive UI, and automated tests.
  • Customer trust — Authenticated actions, explainable fraud decisions, and clear next steps for every failed payment.
  • Demo readiness — Runnable with Docker, seeded scenarios, Swagger docs, and a full walkthrough from login to trip summary.
Looking Ahead

Future Improvements

  • Credit card logic — Support multiple card types, credit limits, repayment tracking, and overseas credit-card controls alongside debit cards.
  • Production authentication — Integrate OAuth 2.0 / OIDC and production-grade customer identity instead of demo authentication.
  • Enterprise integrations — Connect to real card-core, fraud-scoring, and card-network services for enterprise deployment.
