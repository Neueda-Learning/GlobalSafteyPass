# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individual international travellers preparing for a trip, paying abroad, or resolving a card and payment problem while away.

## Product Purpose

Voyage combines trip planning with travel banking so a traveller can check payment readiness before departure, understand spending and currency while abroad, and recover quickly from payment or security incidents.

## Positioning

Banking guidance is organised around each real journey: destination, dates, budget, preferred card, currency support, readiness checks, payments, fraud alerts, and recovery actions share one operating context.

## Operating Context

The product is used one-handed on a phone before departure and while travelling. Important situations include checking a preferred card, reviewing destination currency, finding an ATM, recovering a declined payment, freezing a card, and responding to a fraud alert.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Spring Boot APIs, seeded demonstration data, and customer workflows must remain functional.
- This is a mobile app experience delivered through a responsive web frontend.
- Core areas are journeys, travel payments, cards, readiness, fraud safety, ATM discovery, and support cases.
- Factual banking states and amounts come from the API and must not be invented in the interface.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Voyage. Its voice is calm, direct, protective, and useful under pressure.

## Evidence on Hand

Real product flows and demonstration data are implemented in `src/main/resources/static/app.js` and the Spring services. There are no approved customer claims, testimonials, or performance benchmarks.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the traveller's current journey, not a generic account dashboard.
- Make the next safe action unmistakable.
- Explain financial and security states in plain language.
- Keep emergency actions reachable and reversible.
- Preserve trust through accurate, contextual information.
